          How it works

          1. 1Pick a song

            Search runs on the iTunes catalogue, which has a 30-second preview of almost every track. Songs repeat their chords, so 30 seconds is usually enough to hear the pattern.

          2. 2See its chords

            LiveChord listens to the preview, works out which notes are sounding moment by moment and matches them to the 24 major and minor chords. Changes are smoothed, lined up with the beat, and every chord is drawn as a guitar shape.

          3. 3Play along

            Flip the mic on and play. Four times a second the same matching runs on your guitar, and the green lamp lights when you are on the chord the song is playing, or the one you chose to drill.

          Audio
          Apple's 30-second previews, not whole songs
          Chords
          Major and minor only: no sevenths, suspended or diminished chords
          Shapes
          Standard guitar voicings, open and barre
          Mic
